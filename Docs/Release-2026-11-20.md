# Steel Ball Run demo release — November 20, 2026

The demo uses Unity 6000.4.7f1 and targets Windows x64. Build from the Morioh branch with only `AM3D_MainMenu` and `AM3D_Morioh_Full` enabled, in that order. This checklist supersedes the scene and release instructions in the older roadmap and handoff.

## Scope for the demo

- Keep the existing D4C and Killer Queen selection, keyboard and webcam play, Morioh scenery and obstacles, ordinary and punch bonus coins, Human Tetris, pause/retry/menu flow, results, and offline spoken Stand Coach.
- Defer saved audio settings, choice of webcam, manual recalibration, a dedicated controls panel, and richer handling of tracking loss during a run. Include concise keyboard and webcam instructions with the demo.
- Character power-ups are out of scope. Webcam gestures can pause, resume, and quit during gameplay. Extra obstacle concepts and cosmetic wishlist items are optional.

## Release checks

- [x] Baseline candidate built on Nov 16 from `acf4c27` at `Builds/RC-2026-11-20`. Unity reported 0 errors and 8 warnings. The player stayed up in a headless startup check, and all 400 bundled Stand Coach files matched the local runtime by SHA-256. Live controls, visuals and generated feedback still require a playthrough.
- [ ] Keyboard: launch from the menu, change lanes, jump and duck; collect a regular coin and check scoring. Verify pause freezes the run, then resume, crash, retry, return to the menu, and quit.
- [ ] Webcam: verify calibration, lane changes, jump, crouch, punch bonus and Human Tetris. Verify a missed pose or crash shows results and the webcam overlay does not block their buttons. Retry and confirm that the preview and calibration return.
- [ ] Run speed and gestures: set a fixed speed from the main menu, change it while paused, switch back to Auto, and check that the selection survives a restart. In webcam mode, confirm that both open palms pause, a V sign resumes, and a held thumbs-down quits.
- [ ] Webcam failure: start once with no camera or denied access and once without a visible player. Startup can wait indefinitely for detection/calibration or stop on a camera error without offering a route back. Treat any reproduced lock-in as a release fix; the player should be able to return and select keyboard.
- [ ] Offline Stand Coach: copy the whole build to the demo PC, disconnect the network, then play as each Stand. Crash deliberately, confirm D4C uses Onyx and Killer Queen uses George, and verify the feedback describes the run and gives a useful tip. Check replay, captions, response time and audio levels.
- [ ] Presentation: use the actual projector or demo display and confirm the menu, pause, webcam view and results are readable and clickable at its resolution. Update the showcase to reflect the final game, including Stand Coach.
- [ ] Delivery: confirm the repository link points to the Morioh release branch or release commit. Keep the Windows executable, its DLLs, and the full `_Data` folder together, including `StreamingAssets/StandCoachRuntime`. Leave Unity's `BackUpThisFolder_ButDontShipItWithYourGame` folder out of the package. Include controls, source/setup notes and third-party credits. Review asset rights before distributing the build outside the demo.

Target Nov 17 for playthroughs on the demo hardware, Nov 18 for blocker fixes, Nov 19 for a clean-machine rehearsal and frozen package, and Nov 20 for delivery.
